💎 How to Play: Heist Royale

🎯 Objective & Win Conditions

Compete over 8 intense rounds to escape with the largest fortune, or be the first mastermind to bank 15+ Gold in your safehouse!

⚡ Simultaneous Action & Greed Collisions

Every turn, all players secretly lock in 1 card from their hand. Then all cards reveal simultaneously!

  • 💰 Safecracker: Take 3 Gold from the vault. (If 2 or more players play this, you collide and both get ZERO!)
  • 💎 Cat Burglar: Steal the Giant Diamond (+5 Gold). (Collides and fails if another player plays Cat Burglar!)
  • 🪓 Hacker: Disable traps and steal 1 Gold from every player who scored this turn!
  • 🥷 Pickpocket: Target a rival; if they score gold this turn, steal half of it!
  • 🚨 The Snitch: Call casino SWAT. If 2 or more players collided this turn, collect a +4 Gold bounty from the house!
  • 🚗 Getaway Driver: Permanently bank all your loose gold into your safehouse (banked gold can never be stolen!).
