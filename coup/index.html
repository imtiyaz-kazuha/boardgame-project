📖 How to Play Coup

Coup is a game of bluffing and deception. The last player with any hidden influence cards wins!

🎭 The 5 Characters:
  • 👑 Duke: Take 3 coins from Treasury. Blocks Foreign Aid.
  • 🗡️ Assassin: Pay 3 coins to assassinate an opponent's card. (Can be blocked by Contessa).
  • 📜 Captain: Steal 2 coins from any player. Blocks Stealing.
  • 🛡️ Contessa: Blocks assassination attempts on yourself.
  • 🔍 Ambassador: Draw 2 cards from deck, choose which to keep, and return 2. Blocks Stealing.
🤫 The Golden Rule of Bluffing:

You can claim to have any card when taking an action or blocking, even if you don't actually hold it! But beware: any player can call ⚔️ Challenge! If caught lying, you lose a card. If you were telling the truth, the challenger loses a card!

💥 Launching a Coup:

Pay 7 coins to launch an unblockable, unchallengeable Coup against any target. If you ever reach 10 coins, a Coup is mandatory!
