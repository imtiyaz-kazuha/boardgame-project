📖 How to Play: Connect 4 America

🎯 Objective & Game End

The expedition concludes whenever fewer than 2 unclaimed paths remain on the map (or all routes are exhausted). The player with the highest total score wins!

🗺️ States & Route Points Calculation

The board spans 20 strategic US hub cities across 17 distinct states and districts. Points are calculated dynamically based on real geographic distance:

  • Route Score: \(\approx 1\text{ pt per } 80\text{ km}\) of true Haversine distance (e.g. Seattle to San Francisco is 1,094 km = +14 pts).
  • Shared Quests: Completing shared cross-country bounties awards huge flat bonuses (+12 to +24 pts).

🎫 Transit Tickets, Card Odds & Color Coding

Unclaimed routes on the map display their supported transit types. When drawing cards, the balanced deck odds follow an exact 3 : 5 : 4 : 1 ratio (out of 13 cards):

  • ✈️ Flight Cards (3 / 13 ~ 23%): Used on purple aerial corridors connecting ✈️ Airport Hubs.
  • 🚗 Road Cards (5 / 13 ~ 38%): Used on yellow interstate highway connections.
  • 🚂 Train Cards (4 / 13 ~ 31%): Used on green transcontinental rail corridors.
  • 🃏 Wild Joker (1 / 13 ~ 8%): The ultimate wild card — can substitute for any ticket type!

🔥 High-Stakes Shared Quests (First-to-Claim)

Secret missions are shared public bounties! Both players race to connect the endpoints. The moment one player connects the quest, they claim the bonus points and that quest vanishes forever from the board!

🎲 Team Assignment (Blue vs Red)

Players are randomly assigned to Team Blue 🔵 or Team Red 🔴 at the start of each match. Claimed routes are painted in your team color with distinct patterns (Solid Train, Dashed Road, Dotted Flight)!

🎲 How to Play: Ludo Royale

🎯 Objective

Navigate all 4 of your colored tokens around the 52-tile track and safely down your home runway into the center pocket. The first player to bring all 4 tokens home wins!

🎲 Rolling & Moving

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto your starting tile.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you an immediate bonus roll!
  • Click your highlighted movable token to advance it by the dice value.

⚔️ Capturing & Safe Zones

Landing exactly on an opponent's token sends it back to their yard and grants you a bonus turn! However, Star tiles (⭐) and starting colored tiles are Safe Zones where pieces cannot be captured.

🤖 Autonomous Bots & Multiplayer

Play Solo vs 3 Bots right away, or host a P2P room with any human count (2, 3, or 4). Any seats not taken by humans are automatically played by smart tactical bots!

👑 How to Play: Coup Syndicate

🎯 Objective

Eliminate the influence of all other players and be the last player standing with any secret influence cards remaining.

🎭 The 5 Syndicate Roles

  • 👑 Duke: Take 3 coins from Treasury. Blocks Foreign Aid.
  • 🗡️ Assassin: Pay 3 coins to assassinate another player's card. (Can be blocked by Contessa).
  • 📜 Captain: Steal 2 coins from any rival. Blocks Stealing.
  • 🛡️ Contessa: Blocks assassination attempts on yourself.
  • 🔍 Ambassador: Draw 2 cards from court deck to exchange. Blocks Stealing.

🤫 Bluffing, Challenges & Fast Flow

You can claim to hold any character card, even if you don't actually have it! Any living rival can shout ⚔️ Challenge! within the 10-second timer. If all living players click ✓ Pass or allow the action, the round continues immediately without waiting for the timer to expire!

💥 Launching a Coup

Pay 7 coins to launch an unblockable, unchallengeable Coup against any player to eliminate one of their influence cards. Mandatory if you hold 10+ coins!

🤖 2 to 6 Players & Tactical Bots

Play solo against up to 5 tactical bots, or create a P2P room with friends. Any empty table slots are automatically filled with AI agents that assess bluff probabilities!

🕵️ How to Play: Dancing Criminal

🎯 Objective & Win Conditions

Every player starts with 4 cards. The Criminal card travels secretly between players through card trades and rumor swaps!

  • 🦹 Criminal Victory: If you hold the Criminal and play it as your very last card in hand without getting caught, you and any Accomplice win immediately!
  • 🕵️ Detective Victory: If you play the Detective card (against a target with \(\le 3\) cards) or Police Dog and catch the Criminal holder, the Detective team wins!

🃏 Core Cards & Powers

  • 🦹 Criminal: Can ONLY be played as your final remaining card!
  • 🕵️ Detective: Accuse a suspect with 3 or fewer cards. If correct, Detective wins!
  • 🐕 Police Dog: Sniffs 1 card from a target suspect's hand to see if it is the Criminal.
  • 🤝 Accomplice: You secretly become an ally to the Criminal and win together!
  • 🪓 Alibi: Allows you to claim "Innocent" if accused by the Detective.
  • 🔄 Information Trade: All players simultaneously pass 1 card to the left!
  • 🤫 Rumor: Everyone takes 1 random card from the player on their right.

🤖 Solo with Bots or P2P Multiplayer

Play with 3 to 6 players! Jump straight into a match against intelligent tactical bots, or host a room for friends.

💎 How to Play: Heist Royale (Bank Vault)

🎯 Objective & Win Conditions

Compete over 8 intense rounds to escape with the largest fortune, or be the first mastermind to bank 15+ Gold in your safehouse!

⚡ Simultaneous Action & Greed Collisions

Every turn, all players secretly lock in 1 card from their hand. Then all cards reveal simultaneously!

  • 💰 Safecracker: Take 3 Gold from the vault. (Fails if 2+ players pick Safecracker!)
  • 💎 Cat Burglar: Steal the Giant Diamond (+5 Gold). (Collides and fails if another rogue also picks Cat Burglar!)
  • 🪓 Hacker: EMP blast! Steals 1 Gold from every rogue who successfully scores this turn.
  • 🥷 Pickpocket: Target a rival; if they score gold this turn, snatch half of it!
  • 🚨 The Snitch: Call Bank Security SWAT. If 2+ players collided this turn, collect a +4 Gold bounty from the bank!
  • 🚗 Getaway Driver: Permanently bank all your loose gold into your safehouse (banked gold can never be stolen!).

💎 How to Play: Realm of Legend (Splendor)

🏆 The Victory Objective

Be the first player to reach 15 Prestige Points by recruiting anime champions and winning the favor of legendary Noble patrons!

On Your Turn (Choose EXACTLY 1 Action):

1. Take Gem Tokens from the Bank:

• Take 3 different color gems (e.g. 1 Ruby, 1 Sapphire, 1 Emerald)
• OR take 2 identical color gems (only permitted if 4+ tokens of that color remain in bank).
• Note: Max token pouch hand limit is 10.

2. Recruit an Anime Champion:

Pay the required gem cost. Each recruited card gives you:
• Prestige Points towards the 15 required to win.
• A Permanent Gem Discount! Once recruited, this card permanently reduces all your future card purchases of that element!

3. Reserve a Card:

Hold a card in your private hand (up to 3 held) to prevent your opponent from buying it, and claim 1 Gold Wildcard token from the bank!

⚪⚫ How to Play: Go (Baduk / 碁)

🎯 Objective

Go is an ancient game of territory. Two players (Black and White) take turns placing stones on the grid intersections. The player who surrounds and controls the greatest territory on the board wins.

⚔️ Liberties & Capturing Stones

Every stone or connected chain of stones has adjacent unoccupied intersections called Liberties. If all liberties of an opponent's group are occupied by your stones, the group is captured and removed from the board as prisoners!

🚫 Basic Ko Rule & No Suicide

You cannot make a move that leaves your stone with zero liberties unless that move immediately captures opponent stones (suicide is illegal). The Ko rule prevents endless loops: you cannot immediately recapture a single stone if it repeats the exact previous board state.

🎌 Japanese Rules & Komi

Black moves first. To compensate White for moving second, White receives 6.5 Komi points. When both players pass consecutively, the game enters the removal phase to mark dead stones, and territory is tallied automatically!

⚡ How to Play: Buzzer Clash

⏱️ The 15-Second Buzzer

Each question has a 15-second countdown. First player to tap an answer locks the question! If nobody answers within 15 seconds, the question is automatically skipped and no points are awarded.

🎯 Correct Answer & Speed Points

Answering correctly awards high points based on speed (up to 1,100 pts for immediate buzzes, scaling down with the timer).

💥 Wrong Answer Penalty & 5-Second Steal

If you buzz in and answer wrong, you suffer a -250 point penalty and are locked out. Your opponent gets an intense 5-second Steal Window to claim the points!

🤖 Fallible AI & Custom Settings

Choose between 10, 20, or 30 questions across Cars & Automotive, Geography, History, Science, or All. In Solo mode, the AI simulates real human thinking delay and realistic mistakes (~50% to ~85% accuracy depending on difficulty)!
