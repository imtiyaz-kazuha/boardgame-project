🕵️ How to Play: Dancing Criminal

🎯 Objective & Win Conditions

Every player begins with 4 cards in hand. The Criminal card secretly travels from player to player throughout the match!

  • 🦹 Criminal Victory: If you hold the Criminal and manage to play it as your very last card without being caught, the Criminal (and any Accomplice) wins the game!
  • 🕵️ Detective Victory: If a player uses the Detective card or Police Dog to catch whoever holds the Criminal, the Detective (and innocent civilians) win!

🃏 Key Character Cards

  • 🦹 Criminal: Can ONLY be played when it is your last remaining card. Win instantly if played!
  • 🕵️ Detective: Accuse a suspect with 3 or fewer cards. If they hold the Criminal and have no Alibi, you win!
  • 🐕 Police Dog: Pick a suspect and smell 1 card from their hand. If it is the Criminal, they are caught!
  • 🤝 Accomplice: Secretly allies with the Criminal. You win together if the Criminal escapes!
  • 🪓 Alibi: Can claim "Not guilty" if accused by the Detective, even if you hold the Criminal!
  • 🔄 Information Trade: Everyone simultaneously passes 1 card to the player on their left.
  • 🤫 Rumor / Gossip: Take 1 random card from the player on your right.
  • 🔍 Witness: Peek secretly at a chosen player's entire hand.
